The only rule that matters

Show up 3 times a week for 6 weeks. That's it. You will see and feel a real difference. Don't worry about doing it perfectly — consistency beats perfection every time.

The golden rule for beginners
When 3×12 feels easy → add 5 lbs

Every time you finish all 3 sets of 12 reps and it feels genuinely easy, add 5 lbs next session. That's how you get stronger. It's that simple.
